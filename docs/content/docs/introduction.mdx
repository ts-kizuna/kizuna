## The idea

Your server, your clients, and every generated artifact read from one contract. In real projects you split routes and schemas across files and compose them. See [organizing the contract](/docs/building/contract#splitting-routes-across-files).

## Inside a handler

The handler receives whatever the contract declares, validated and typed.
